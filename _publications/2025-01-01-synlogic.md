This work presents SynLogic, a method for generating large-scale, verifiable reasoning datasets to train large language models for better logical reasoning performance. The paper demonstrates that synthetic reasoning data generated via SynLogic significantly improves LLM performance on logical reasoning benchmarks and beyond.
